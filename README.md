## About the course

This course introduces generative AI, agentic AI and autonomous AI agents, and the security, governance and ethical risks they bring to customer-service and hospitality settings. You work entirely on ready-made websites, chatbots and AI agents, so **no coding is required**. All activity data is fictional.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

1. Demonstrate generative AI concepts and applications relevant to customer service and hospitality management.
2. Apply prompt engineering techniques and analyse output variations to improve generative AI performance in service settings.
3. Identify ethical risks and analyse bias in AI-generated content used in customer engagement.

## Topics covered

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Topic 1: Generative AI, Agentic AI and AI Agents** | A short history of AI from 2023 to 2026, how generative AI works, context engineering, the agentic loop and harness engineering, OpenClaw and Hermes, skills, tools and multi-agent systems |
| **Topic 2: Prompt Engineering and Post-Training for Autonomous AI Agents** | Running the Hermes agent on a MiniMax model, prompt-engineering principles, analysing data and building presentations, and installing tools and skills to improve output |
| **Topic 3: Security Risk of Autonomous AI Agents** | AI data governance and accountability, brand risk, job impact and redesign, AI-agent cybersecurity risks, advanced threats and a safe roll-out framework |
