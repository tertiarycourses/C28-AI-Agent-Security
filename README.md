## About the course

An AI agent cannot reliably tell your instructions from an attacker's. In the morning you see how always-on AI agents work: a brief history of AI, OpenClaw, Hermes Agent and hosted agents compared, and five ways people and agents work together. Then you break one. On a Hermes agent running on a free model, you plant a prompt injection in a customer review, jailbreak it with a comedy skit and watch an ordinary request leak fictional member data. You fix each one and measure the difference.

In the afternoon you govern AI agents with the five principles of AI security: Data Control, Permission Control, Human in the Loop, Traceability and Right-Sized Models. An interview prompt helps you write an AI Governance Policy, and mock traces of a leak three months ago become an AI Incident Report under Singapore's PDPA. All lab data belongs to Kopi Kaki Co., a fictional Singapore kopi startup. No coding is needed.

## Learning outcomes

- Demonstrate generative AI concepts and applications relevant to customer service and hospitality management.
- Apply prompt engineering techniques and analyse output variations to improve generative AI performance in service settings.
- Identify ethical risks and analyse bias in AI-generated content used in customer engagement.

## Topics covered

1. **Human-AI Collaboration and Always-On AI Agents.** From rules to learning to action; agentic AI versus AI agents; how an agent works like a person; how generative AI writes; generative versus discriminative models; five collaboration scenarios; use cases; OpenClaw to Hermes to hosted always-on agents, compared.
2. **AI Agent Vulnerabilities: Prompt Injection, Jailbreak and Data Leak.** Prompt engineering and data quality; why a model cannot tell a genuine prompt from a malicious one; jailbreak techniques; personal data and the PDPA.
3. **AI Governance and Security: The Five Principles of AI Security.** How a model works; limits, bias and ethics; the five principles with examples; IMDA, NIST, OWASP, ISO and the PDPA; incident response.
